# Initial Hero Class

## Squire

The first prototype Hero class is **Squire**.

Squire is the foundational adventurer class.

It represents inexperienced recruits, militia trainees, retainers, and aspiring heroes who have not yet developed into more specialized roles.

Every Squire gains the following bonus Enhancement:

> **Throw Rock**
> This hero may perform a combat action without a Weapon equipped.

Throw Rock is granted by the Squire class and does not count against the hero’s limit of two acquired Enhancements.

Throw Rock ensures that a Squire remains capable of contributing to combat after unequipping a Weapon or when assigned two non-Weapon Equipment cards.

It also establishes an initial distinction between:

* actions available to all heroes
* actions granted by Class
* actions enabled or improved by Equipment

The ability should remain intentionally modest.

Throw Rock prevents complete helplessness without making Weapons unnecessary.

# Class Enhancements

Classes may grant one or more bonus Enhancements.

Class Enhancements:

* are printed as part of the Class
* do not count against the hero’s two acquired Enhancement slots
* remain available regardless of Equipment
* establish the class’s basic mechanical identity

Classes should initially provide only a small number of simple rules.

The purpose of Class is to create recognizable starting behavior without turning each class into a complete character subsystem.

For the prototype:

> **Squire**
> Bonus Enhancement: Throw Rock
